WHY THIS IS THE PRIORITY AND NOT COSMETIC
================================================================================

The overseer has repeatedly logged an anomaly it could not explain:

  **The E2E Gate reports `success` while `26-fleet-dns-tls` fails with
  "hard failure under RUN_LIVE_REGRESSION".**

That has been treated as a mystery and re-reported for days. A numeric selector that silently runs
TWO unrelated suites is a first-class candidate explanation: one suite's failure can land inside
another's summary, or a selector can run a suite the caller never intended and attribute its result
to the wrong name.

**DO NOT ASSUME THAT IS THE CAUSE. PROVE OR DISPROVE IT.** It is a hypothesis with good motive, not
a finding. If the collision turns out NOT to explain the green-rollup-with-hard-failure, say so
plainly and keep digging - a wrong confident answer here is worse than an open question, because it
would close a real defect that is still live.
