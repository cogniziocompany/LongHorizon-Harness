## SCOPE

### 1. The per-tick process, written down
Produce a short, concrete procedure the overseer runs **every tick**, cheap enough to actually run:
 - **Recall step:** query memory-mcp (`memory-recall`, `source: "overseer"`) for turns since the last
   tick, across ALL devices - not just this one.
 - **Discovery step:** surface sessions active in the last N hours that the overseer has NOT already
   accounted for, with their project/repo and last-activity time. **N must default to at least 24
   hours** - the failure above happened inside a single day.
 - **Triage step:** classify each as (a) a handoff/plan the overseer is expected to execute,
   (b) informational, (c) already captured as a queued task. **Only (a) needs action.**
 - **The rule that matters:** *if a plan names a session id or a scratchpad path, GO AND READ IT.*
   Never ask a human to paste something that is on disk and addressable.
