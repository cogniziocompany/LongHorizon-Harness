================================================================================
THE DIAGNOSIS - already proven, do not re-derive it
================================================================================

`https://billing.easybutt0n.ai/admin` serves Cloudflare **502 on roughly half of all requests**.
Two plausible theories are both WRONG and were eliminated: the hostname resolves and HAS an ingress
rule (rule `2` on tunnel `c5fdb916-947d-4440-95b9-88742bc4dcb0`), and the origin is healthy -
`mcp-billing-service` on CT100 was up 15h, `healthy`, image `sha-728a140`, and
`http://192.168.21.153:20251/api/health/ready` returns **200**.

The real cause is two things combining:

1. **A duplicate connector on the PROD tunnel.** `/opt/mcp-cognizioware-uat/.env` line 6 sets
   `TUNNEL_TOKEN` to a token that decodes to `"t":"c5fdb916-947d-4440-95b9-88742bc4dcb0"` - the
   **prod** tunnel. So `billing-uat-lane-cloudflared-1` is a SECOND connector on prod. (The dev
   lane is correct, on its own tunnel `b36c4501-88ac-481b-9005-10bc87c4d146`.)
   The token came from the committed `cloudflare/uat.env`, which is **labelled "uat" but holds the
   PROD token** - exactly the mistake `design/handoffs/billing-litellm-mcp-next-export.md:76`
   warns against.

2. **A container-name origin that only resolves from one connector.** Ingress rule `2` points at
   `http://mcp-billing-service:20251`, a Docker DNS name - every OTHER rule on that tunnel uses a
   host IP. `cloudflared-uat` sits on `mcp-cognizioware-networkk` where that name resolves;
   `billing-uat-lane-cloudflared-1` sits on `billing-uat-lane_default` where it does **not**.
   **Whichever connector Cloudflare happens to pick decides whether you get 200 or 502.** That is
   the coin-flip.
